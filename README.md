# Dad Joke Showdown

A single dad joke for the make.fun bounty "Dad Joke Showdown": a father asks his son why he brought a ladder to dinner, and the boy answers that he heard the steaks were high — a clean steaks/stakes pun, told in two beats, with the punchline landing last and every word earning its place.

> I asked my son why he brought a ladder to dinner. He said he heard the steaks were high.

## How to run

```bash
python3 main.py
```

This writes the entry joke to `/output/entry.txt` (no trailing newline) and validates that it is 1–200 characters long.
